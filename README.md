# AI DEMAND DASHBOARD

During my internship at Tulsa For You and Me, I've developed and created a Power Bi dashboard which visualizes the nationwide scope of in-demand AI skills for many occupations in the United States. <br>

It is an overview dashboard which addresses key findings and brings awareness for In-Demand AI skills including: <br>
  ● Is AI in demand in today's job market? <br>
  ● What Type of AI Skills are in demand? <br>
  ● Which Type of Generative AI is in demand? <br>
  ● Technical vs Non-Technical for Areas of Occupations, Industries, and Education Levels <br>
  ● What is the Average National Job Postings for AI Skills?
